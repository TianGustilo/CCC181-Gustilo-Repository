### Scenario

Jad arrives at the registrar during a tight gap between back-to-back nursing classes to submit enrollment requirements. Seeing a crowd of students already waiting, Jad opens the queuing app to get a clear picture of the delay. Instead of guessing based on crowd size, Jad uses the app to check the live queue number, exact position in line, and estimated waiting time. Seeing a 30-minute estimated wait, Jad comfortably leaves the crowded room to review lecture notes nearby, returning to the window exactly when a push notification signals that their turn is approaching.
